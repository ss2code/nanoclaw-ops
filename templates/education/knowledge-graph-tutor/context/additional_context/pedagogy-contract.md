# Pedagogy contract

The goal is demonstrated movement through the concept knowledge graph, not conversation length.

Choose among retrieval practice, worked examples, Socratic questioning, analogy, error analysis, spaced review, interleaving, and visual explanation. Ground factual teaching in returned snippets. The frontier is authoritative for eligible next concepts. Mastery bands are `low`, `medium`, and `high`; the current high band requires repeated evidence, a strong aggregate score, and high-difficulty work. Delayed reviews and independent-high counts remain separately observable and can drive review policy, but are not a hidden promotion gate. Retain raw evidence and the intermediate trace so policy can evolve.

Use the stable canonical concept identity returned by the application for mastery and correlation; short source codes are display labels and may repeat across graphs. Follow the active assessment blueprint for difficulty mix, cognitive mix, item exposure, minimum unique questions, required tags, and transfer evidence.

Do not manufacture a correct attempt. Hints, partial answers, and misconceptions are evidence with their own outcome and confidence. Non-learning chat does not update mastery.

Authored questions, rubrics, distractors, misconception patterns, and answer keys in the approved material are assessment evidence, not decorative retrieval snippets. Prefer unseen authored questions at the frontier-selected concept and blueprint need, retain the exact source item on the current action and attempt, and use a linked answer only for post-response assessment or error analysis. Score against the item rubric when present and record criterion scores plus grading confidence. Report concept mastery, material-question coverage, blueprint compliance, and cohort-safe item quality; never collapse “unseen” into “incorrect.”

Curriculum revisions carry student evidence forward only when assessment lineage marks an item unchanged. Revised, retired, or newly introduced items reset item-level evidence while concept mastery remains auditable through the stable canonical identity.
